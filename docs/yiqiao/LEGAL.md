# Licensing, Provenance, and Third-Party Components

[简体中文](LEGAL.zh-CN.md) | **English**

YiQiao is an independently maintained and released open-source product under
the Apache License 2.0. Inherited code, YiQiao modifications, and third-party
components shipped in release artifacts retain their respective copyright,
provenance, and modification records.

The following English files are the authoritative legal records distributed
with YiQiao:

- [LICENSE](../../LICENSE): the complete Apache License 2.0 text.
- [NOTICE](../../NOTICE): copyright and provenance notices that must accompany
  redistribution.
- [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md): third-party components
  and their licensing obligations.
- [MODIFICATIONS.md](../../MODIFICATIONS.md): the generated and verified index
  of upstream-derived files carrying the prominent modification notices
  required by Apache License 2.0 section 4(b).

This overview explains the license structure but does not replace those
authoritative files. Source redistributions must retain the root legal records
and the prominent modification notices embedded in source files. Python
distributions carry the legal records in package metadata, while container
images carry them under `/usr/share/licenses/yiqiao/`; redistributors must
preserve the applicable payload for each artifact type.

Product UI, installation instructions, images, service names, environment
variables, and new public interfaces use YiQiao exclusively. Original project
names, dependency names, and historical paths in legal records exist only for
attribution and traceability; they do not imply branding, sponsorship, or
endorsement.
